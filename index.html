PIEAS robotic arm

Final-year project, 2024. Our team developed the embedded control system for a six-axis manipulator. An STM32 master computed joint targets and sent them over CAN to six motor controllers. We checked the kinematics in MATLAB and CoppeliaSim before testing the hardware.

Ongoing robotics work. I extended the arm model in ROS 2, Gazebo and MoveIt for conveyor sorting and apple harvesting. In the orchard experiment, a trained appearance classifier grades each apple, depth estimates its position and LiDAR checks the observed surface. The simulated arm picked ripe, unripe and visibly damaged apples and placed each in its matching basket.
